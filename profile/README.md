Built for transparency. For everyone.

Open Bodycam is a body-worn camera designed for individuals, developers, and researchers.

"You are in control". Body cameras shouldn't require blind trust. The Open Bodycam project is not about surveillance, it's about giving everyone the ability to explore, document and research the reality.

Visit [official website](https://open-bodycam.xtl.tw/) for news and more information!
